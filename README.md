## 🚀 Overview

Creating engaging YouTube videos starts with a solid blueprint. The **YouTube Script to Storyboard Agent** bridges the gap between text and visualization. By leveraging advanced LLMs and agentic workflows, this tool parses your video scripts, breaks them down into scene-by-scene beats, suggests visual descriptions, camera angles, on-screen text, and audio cues, streamlining the pre-production process for content creators and video editors.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Automated Scene Breakdown:** Intelligently splits a raw script into logical scenes, segments, or timestamps.
- **Visual Prompt Generation:** Crafts detailed scene descriptions and AI image generation prompts (e.g., for Midjourney, DALL-E) for every single shot.
- **Cinematography Recommendations:** Suggests professional camera angles, movements, transitions, and pacing cues.
- **Audio & B-Roll Cues:** Recommends sound effects (SFX), background music shifts, and appropriate B-Roll footage.
- **Export Capabilities:** Easily export your generated storyboard into structured formats (JSON, Markdown, CSV, etc.) for your editing team.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Requirements

- **Language:** Python 3.10+
- **Frameworks / Libraries:** LangChain / LlamaIndex (or custom agent logic)
- **AI Models:** OpenAI GPT-4 / Anthropic Claude (configurable)
